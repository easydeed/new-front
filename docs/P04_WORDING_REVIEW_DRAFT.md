# Draft retention wording for owner and counsel review

**Not approved for publication.** P04 D7 authorizes preparation only. No website, privacy/terms page, customer email or external communication is changed by this draft.

## Draft expiry explanation

An unconfirmed draft expires after seven days. Its confirmation link no longer permits approval, and preview bytes are cleared by the lifecycle process, subject to authorized retention holds. Expiry does not delete the entire request record or its history. Marked public demos have a separate cleanup rule.

## Draft optional contact explanation

Optional approver email is kept separately from the document and approval evidence, and is not mailed in v1. The current operating policy makes eligible new-record contact due for removal 90 days after recorded approval, rejection or expiry, subject to authorized holds. Actual job coverage and treatment of legacy copies must be verified before any deadline or complete-erasure promise is published.

## Draft evidence and backup explanation

Document bytes and recorded approval evidence are distinct from optional contact data. A recorded name, role or license is a claim, not identity or license verification. Historical backups or separately governed records may contain information subject to their own retention, recovery and hold rules. Final durations, scope, exceptions and deletion-request procedures require counsel approval and a verified inventory; this draft supplies no missing backup-expiry number.
