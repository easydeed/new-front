One file, start to finish

From the address to the printed packet, without retyping a thing.

Here is a real file as it moves across your desk. Every step below is something you do today by hand.

01 · Open the file
Start with what is already on your desk.
Type the property address, or upload the preliminary title report. County records and the prelim fill in the APN, legal description and owner of record. Each value shows where it came from, so you know what you are trusting.
02 · Make the calls
The decisions stay yours, with the context beside them.
Vesting is proposed, never assumed. Transfer tax is worked out for the county and the city, and each exemption explains what it covers before you choose it. The software does arithmetic. You make the legal choices.
03 · Check it
Two checks run before anything prints.
First, is the deed complete. Second, does the page match what the recorder publishes: margins, type and the return-address block. Anything off is shown in plain words while you can still fix it.
04 · Print
One click prints the whole packet.
The deed, the notary acknowledgment page, the PCOR and the BOE form, all filled from the answers you already confirmed. If something changes later, the correction stays linked to the document it replaced.

What officers ask before they try it.

Straight answers, including the ones where the answer is not yet.

Do I need IT to set this up?
No. It runs in your browser. Create an account and you are on your first file.
What if the recorder rejects it?
Formatting is measured against what the county publishes and shown to you before you print. Acceptance is always the recorder’s decision, and we will not claim otherwise.
Who is responsible for the deed?
You are, exactly as today. That is why nothing prints until you have confirmed every field, and why the record shows that you did.
Does it connect to SoftPro, Qualia or ResWare?
Not today. Property details come from a county lookup or a prelim you upload, and the rest is entered once.
Can my assistant prep and I review?
Not yet. One login owns its own work today. Shared office files are what the Business plan is for, and it is not on sale until it works.
Can I stop halfway and come back?
Yes. Your progress saves automatically and the file is waiting when you return.
Is my client’s information safe?
Sessions are encrypted and expire. Every PDF is fingerprinted when it is created. All eight outside services we use are named on the trust page.
What does Free leave out?
Nothing in the product. All 19 documents, PCOR and BOE included. Professional adds priority support.

Today the answer is info@deedpro.io. Add hours, a response time and a phone number; for an officer on a deadline it is worth more than any feature.
