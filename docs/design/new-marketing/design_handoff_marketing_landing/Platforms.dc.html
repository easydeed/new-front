The questions your CTO and your counsel will ask. Answered now.

Including the ones where the answer is no. You'd find out anyway; better here than in week three.

Who is responsible if a deed is wrong?
The professional who approved it, which is exactly why approval is mandatory and recorded. DeedPro formats and records. It is software, not a law firm, and gives no legal advice.
Is there an uptime guarantee?
No SLA and no status page. Nothing measures uptime yet. If your contract needs either, say so on the call and you get a straight answer.
Will you break my integration?
Additive changes ship inside v1. Breaking changes go to a new path, /api/v2, with a public changelog. We changed v1 once, before anyone integrated, to remove the path that skipped the human.
What do you keep, and for how long?
An unconfirmed draft is deleted after seven days. Not archived, deleted. Approver contact details are purged on a retention schedule. Public verification never exposes parties, address or APN. All eight outside services that touch your data are named on the trust page.
Rate limits and volume?
Hourly and daily ceilings per key, reported in response headers. Tell us your expected volume and we set the ceiling to match.
Only California?
Yes, and that focus is why the formatting is right. Need a second state today? We'll say so on the first call and save you the month.
Why 7 instruments over the API and 19 in the app?
Affidavits and declarations need a hand at the moment of signing, so they stay in the app by design. Two deed types are paused for review and listed in the changelog.
SOC 2? Penetration test? DPA?
No SOC 2 report and no penetration test scheduled. A data processing agreement is drafted and pending counsel; ask and you get the draft with that caveat. Questionnaires go to info@deedpro.io.
Who do I call when something breaks?NEEDS YOUR ANSWER
Today the answer is info@deedpro.io. Add hours, a response time and a named person; it will close more deals than any feature.
What happens to my deeds if I leave?NEEDS YOUR ANSWER
Export and retention after cancellation. State it plainly; it removes the fear of lock-in.
